After you're finished with the introduction, you can then begin with your model replacement! Here you will find the basic processes of replacing skeletons (player models, npcs, etc), replacing static models (equipment, objects, etc), and replacing entire scenes. If you find yourself wanting to go further to improve your model replacements, feel free to check out the [advanced guides](/docs/2ship2harkinian/mod-development/models/advanced/index.md) in the following section.

- [Player Model Guide](/docs/2ship2harkinian/mod-development/models/basics/player-models.md) - Replacing Link's models
